Codebook Check

Paste your codebook and this page tells you what the assignment asks for that your draft does not have yet. It checks structure. It cannot tell you whether a definition is a good definition, and it does not grade.

Nothing is uploaded. The check runs in this page, on your machine. No account, no server, no network request once the page has loaded.

Results

What it checks

  • A unit of analysis stated once, and not left as the template placeholder
  • At least three variables, each with a conceptual and an operational definition
  • A level of measurement on every variable, and one of the four valid values
  • At least two substantive categories plus a catch-all, so the scheme is exhaustive
  • A description on every category, because a label is not a rule
  • Category pairs that may overlap, which is the failure that destroys reliability
  • Decision rules, and whether there are fewer than one per variable
  • Examples, and which categories have none
  • Template boilerplate and placeholders left in the file

Built for the codebook template in the V2V student workspace. It serves MC 451 and MC 501 Codebook assignments and MC 427 Module 4.
