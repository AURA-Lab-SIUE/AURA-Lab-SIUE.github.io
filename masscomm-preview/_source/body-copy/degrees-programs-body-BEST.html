Programs

The Department of Mass Communications offers undergraduate and graduate programs accredited by ACEJMC — a distinction held by fewer than one in four journalism and mass communication programs nationwide. Re-accredited in 2024; next review in 2030.

Undergraduate program

The Bachelor of Arts or Bachelor of Science in Mass Communications prepares students for careers across the media landscape through three professional tracks:

Journalism

Reporting, editing, news production, and digital media across platforms.

Careers: reporter, editor, news producer, podcast producer, digital content manager.

Advertising & Strategic Media

Campaigns, branding, public relations, and media planning.

Careers: account executive, media planner, PR specialist, social media manager, brand strategist.

Media Production

Video, audio, and digital content creation for broadcast and beyond.

Careers: video producer, editor, cinematographer, audio engineer, motion graphics designer.

Graduate program

The Master of Science in Media Studies offers two concentrations:

  • Professional Media Practice — media message design, media policy, and media management
  • Media Studies — media and politics, media influence, media ethics, media literacy, transnational media, and media and representation

We also offer a certificate in Digital Media Literacy, and an Accelerated Combined BA/BS + MS option that allows qualified undergraduates to begin graduate coursework early.

Graduate assistantships

A limited number of graduate assistantships are available to qualified students each year. Assistantships typically include a tuition waiver and stipend in exchange for research or teaching support. Contact the Graduate Program Director for current availability.

Recent graduates have applied their degrees in television journalism, advertising, public relations, sports marketing, and public policy. Others have gone on to pursue law and doctoral degrees.
