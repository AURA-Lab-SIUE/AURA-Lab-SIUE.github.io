The Department of Mass Communications is accredited by the highly selective Accrediting Council on Education in Journalism and Mass Communication. Our curriculum prepares students to be responsive to a fast-paced, ever-changing professional environment — choosing one of three professional options: Journalism, Media Production, or Advertising & Strategic Media.
