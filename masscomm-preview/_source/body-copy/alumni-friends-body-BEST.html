Support Mass Communications

Every gift we receive from our alumni and friends — regardless of the amount — is transformational. These generous contributions fund scholarships and equipment that directly impact current students' education and career preparation.

Technology investment

The department has invested over $500,000 in facility upgrades since 2018, including a renovated multimedia lab, new audio/podcasting lab, LED studio lighting, and updated computer labs. Your support helps us maintain professional-grade facilities for the next generation of media professionals.

To support our technology fund, give to the Mass Communications Lab Development fund and search for '4331 - Mass Communications Lab Development.'
