The Department of Mass Communications maintains professional-grade production facilities in Dunham Hall. The department has invested over $800,000 in facility renovations and equipment since 2025.

Our facilities

Television Studio

Multi-camera HD studio with a professional news set, Ross Carbonite switcher, and ETC digital lighting.

Video Editing Lab

10 Adobe Creative Cloud workstations connected to 48TB shared storage for collaborative editing.

Audio Lab

Renovated audio production and podcasting lab with 4 workstations, Adobe Audition, and 2 control rooms.

Multimedia Lab

Smart classroom in Dunham Hall 1014 with 16 Mac workstations and Adobe Creative Cloud.

Field Cameras & Equipment

19 HD cameras, Nikon DSLRs, 18 lighting kits, audio gear, and tripods available for checkout.

Video Services

Commercial production from concept through completion for internal and external clients.

Production engineers

Our production engineers, Ben Moyer and Theresa Pauli, maintain all department equipment and facilities, handle repairs, and oversee the equipment checkout process for students.
