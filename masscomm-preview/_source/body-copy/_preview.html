The Department of Mass Communications prepares students for careers across the media landscape — journalism, advertising, strategic communications, media production and digital media — through three professional tracks built on a shared foundation in writing, ethics, critical thinking, and media law.

Mission

The Department of Mass Communications at SIUE is committed to free expression and providing students with opportunities to meaningfully engage with the world around them through liberal arts education and professional skills development. We foster creative, critical and independent thinking. We teach our students to tell compelling stories about people and communities that are fair, truthful, and accurate.

Vision

Greater national and global recognition through community engagement and innovative curriculum that produces well-rounded students who can succeed in a variety of jobs.

Department overview

Located in Dunham Hall on the Edwardsville campus, the department offers state-of-the-art facilities including a television studio, audio production labs, a multimedia lab, and professional field equipment.

Our graduates work at organizations including the St. Louis Post-Dispatch, ESPN, CBS, Anheuser-Busch, and Fleishman-Hillard. The department holds ACEJMC accreditation and was most recently re-accredited in 2024.
