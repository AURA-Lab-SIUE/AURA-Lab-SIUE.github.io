Health Communication

Elaboration Likelihood Model

ELM · Petty & Cacioppo, 1986 · 4-scene explainer

Central route Peripheral route Elaboration Motivation & ability Attitude strength
01

The fork in the road

A message arrives and the audience faces two paths. The scene opens on a single line splitting into two glowing branches.

02

The central route

When motivation and ability are high, viewers travel the thinking path: the argument's evidence is weighed and a durable attitude forms.

03

The peripheral route

When attention is low, the shortcut path lights up instead. Surface cues like a likeable source stand in for real reasoning.

04

Why it matters

Attitudes built on the central route resist change, so durable health campaigns work to raise both motivation and ability.
