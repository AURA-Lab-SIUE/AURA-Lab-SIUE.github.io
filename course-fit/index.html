Does this career belong on the list?

What this list is. These are the careers Career Pathfinder offers students as Mass Communications outcomes. Choose one and the tool shows Illinois wage data, the software that work expects, and the courses that prepare for it.

Where it came from, and why we are asking. It was assembled from federal occupation data, not by this department. Nobody here has ever checked it. So some entries may be a stretch for our graduates, and obvious ones may be missing entirely.

What keep and drop do. Drop removes it from what students are shown. Keep is a vote to defend it if someone else drops it. Most rows should stay untouched, and untouched means no opinion, not agreement.

The greyed titles under each career are the job titles students will actually meet in postings. They are what a search returns, so judge the career by them as much as by its formal name.
