# Retired 2026-09-07 — AURA Lab logo and icon system

These files are retired, not deleted. They are kept for provenance only.

SIUE's Visual Identity Requirements (effective 2026-09-01) state that
departments and offices may not create or use their own logos, icons, seals
or visual identities (p.7), and that secondary logos, icons, emblems and
unofficial visual identities are not permitted (p.8). AURA Lab is not among
the approved exceptions listed on p.23.

Do not reuse anything in this folder. Official artwork must be requested from
University Marketing and Communications (marketing@siue.edu).
